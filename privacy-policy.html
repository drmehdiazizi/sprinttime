SprintTime Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Introduction

SprintTime is a sprint timing and video analysis application that helps users measure sprint performance from recorded videos.

Video Access

SprintTime requires access to videos selected by the user to analyze sprint performance, measure times, and create exported videos.

User videos are processed locally on the device. SprintTime does not upload user videos to any external server.

Data Collection

SprintTime does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information.

Permissions

Photo and video permissions are used only for selecting videos for sprint analysis and saving exported videos.

Third-Party Services

SprintTime does not share user data with third parties.
